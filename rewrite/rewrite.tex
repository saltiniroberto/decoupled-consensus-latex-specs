\section{Common substrate}\label{sec:substrate}

A fixed validator set~$V$ is given.  Slot~$s$ starts at $t_s=4\Delta s$ and
has an assigned proposer and a fixed committee $\committee_s\subseteq V$; in
every committee, honest members outnumber adversarial members.
After~$t_{\rm GST}$, $\Delta$ is a strict delivery bound.  A scheduled action
uses the store immediately before its public time.

Every block~$B$ has a root~$B.\rootf$, slot~$B.\slot$, parent~$B.\parent$,
and proposer index~$B.\proposer$.
Processed blocks form a parent-closed tree rooted at~$\genesis$.  Write
$B\preceq C$ when $B=C$ or $B$ is an ancestor of~$C$, and write $B\prec C$
for strict ancestry.  Two blocks are compatible when one is an ancestor of
the other, and they conflict otherwise.
The depth of a block is the number of parent edges from genesis.  ``Deepest''
means maximum depth; ties use a fixed root order.

$\Sigma.\timestamp(x)$ is the time at which object~$x$ is processed into the
store.

\section{Goldfish available chain}\label{sec:goldfish}

This section defines block-only Goldfish: the store, view merge, one-slot fork
choice, and available confirmation.  Later sections extend this store
and reuse these handler names: a later layer may add lines or replace a
handler body outright, and
Section~\ref{sec:complete-protocol} gives every handler in its final
form.

\subsection{Schedule and wire objects}\label{sec:goldfish-schedule}

Slot~$s$ has these public actions:
\[
\begin{array}{c@{\qquad}l}
 t_s             & \text{proposal},\\
 t_s+\Delta      & \text{Goldfish vote},\\
 t_s+2\Delta     & \text{support cutoff},\\
 t_s+3\Delta     & \text{view freeze},\\
 t_s+6\Delta     & \text{slot-$s$ confirmation evaluation}.
\end{array}
\]
Because $t_s+6\Delta=t_{s+1}+2\Delta$, the last action is also the support
action of slot~$s+1$.

A Goldfish vote is a tuple $(\valindex,s,B)$ from validator
$\valindex\in\committee_s$ with
head~$B$, where $B.\slot\leq s$.  A block~$B$ carries $B.\parent$,
$B.\slot$, $B.\proposer$, a set $B.\mathsf{votes}$ of
slot-$(B.\slot-1)$ Goldfish votes, and a subset
$B.\mathsf{support\_votes}\subseteq B.\mathsf{votes}$.
An honest proposer puts every previous-slot vote it holds in
$B.\mathsf{votes}$ and puts exactly the members resolved at proposal time in
$B.\mathsf{support\_votes}$.  A slot-$s$ block is valid only when
$B.\proposer=\operatorname{proposer}(s)$.  There is no proposal envelope:
the block is the only wire object a proposer emits, and $B.\mathsf{votes}$ is
the channel by which a proposal forwards votes.  The support subset records
the proposer's view; it is not a second set of votes to process.

\subsection{Store}\label{sec:goldfish-store}

\begin{definition}[Store]\label{def:store}
The store is
\[
\begin{split}
 \Sigma=(&t,s,\T,\timestamp[\cdot],\gfPool[\cdot],\\
  &\liveConfirmed,\latestConfirmed).
\end{split}
\]
$\Sigma.\T$ is the tree of processed blocks and $\Sigma.\gfPool[k]$ the set
of processed slot-$k$ votes.  Both are timestamped.
$\Sigma.\gfPool[k]$ keeps at most two distinct votes per validator; no rule
needs a third pool entry.  $\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$ is the block the last evaluated
slot confirmed. $\Sigma.\latestConfirmed$ is the
monotone record the node exposes; no rule in this protocol reads it.

Initially $\Sigma.\T=\{\genesis\}$,
$\Sigma.\timestamp(\genesis)=-\infty$, and
$\Sigma.\liveConfirmed=\Sigma.\latestConfirmed=\genesis$; other fields are
empty.
\end{definition}

The store keeps messages and their timestamps, and nothing else. Every rule
below is a timestamp comparison on this one pool.  A validator has equivocated
as of~$t$ when $\Sigma.\gfPool[k]$ holds two of its votes both timestamped
before~$t$, so its \emph{equivocation-detection time} is the later of the
two timestamps, and one cutoff decides which votes are timely and which
equivocations are.

$\Call{on\_block}{\Sigma,B}$ and
$\Call{on\_goldfish\_vote}{\Sigma,\mathit{vote}}$
accept each object at most once.  A call that returns before acceptance does
not consume the object: a later delivery may invoke the handler again after
the guard condition changes.  A block is processed after its parent.  A vote is
processed on receipt, whether or not the store holds its head; a vote is
\emph{resolved} when it does.  For a vote with head~$H$, the
\emph{resolution time} is the derived function
\[
 \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})=
 \begin{cases}
  \max\{\Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote}),\Sigma.\timestamp(H)\},
    & H\in\Sigma.\T,\\
  +\infty, & H\notin\Sigma.\T.
 \end{cases}
\]

An honest node forwards every block it admits and every accepted Goldfish
vote independently.  The vote rule also applies when the node first accepts
the vote from an admitted block.  The node ignores votes older than the
previous slot or from a future slot.  It may discard
expired pool entries.

\subsection{View merge}\label{sec:view-merge}

To run the Goldfish fork choice in slot~$s$, a voter at $t_s+\Delta$ forms
two sets.  The set $\mathit{votes}$ contains each slot-$(s-1)$ vote that the
store received before $t_{s-1}+3\Delta$, together with every slot-$(s-1)$
vote included in an admitted slot-$s$ proposal.  The subset
$\mathit{support\_votes}$ contains local votes resolved before the freeze time
$t_{s-1}+3\Delta$, together with each vote in the proposal's
$B.\mathsf{support\_votes}$ whose head is in $\Sigma.\T$ by voting time.  This
proposal arm does not require the vote to have entered $\Sigma.\gfPool$ or to
have a local receipt timestamp.  For a proposal~$B$, a vote in
$B.\mathsf{votes}\setminus B.\mathsf{support\_votes}$ remains
outside $\mathit{support\_votes}$ even if it resolves before the voter runs.  Participation
and equivocation are determined by $\mathit{votes}$, regardless of resolution,
while $\mathit{support\_votes}$ determines direct subtree support.

The proposer does not apply the freeze.  It uses all previous-slot votes it
holds when it builds the block as $\mathit{votes}$ and their resolved subset as
$\mathit{support\_votes}$, and carries both sets in the proposal.  Voters do
not derive the proposal's support subset again from their later stores.

\subsection{Goldfish fork-choice}\label{sec:one-slot-ghost}

\begin{definition}[Goldfish score and walk]\label{def:goldfish-walk}
Fix a vote slot~$s$, a set $\mathit{votes}$ of slot-$s$ votes, and a subset
$\mathit{support\_votes}\subseteq\mathit{votes}$.
Validator $v\in\committee_s$ \emph{equivocates} when
$\mathit{votes}$ holds two of its distinct votes, and \emph{participates}
when it holds at least one.
$\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s,B}$ counts
every equivocator plus every non-equivocating participant for which
$\mathit{support\_votes}$ holds a vote whose head descends from~$B$.  An
equivocator counts for every block and stays among the participants:
discovering an equivocation cannot make a majority-eligible block
ineligible, though it can make another block newly eligible.  A non-equivocating validator counts
once, in one subtree.

Throughout this document, we use this as a building block:
\[
 \Call{ghost}
 {\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},\mathit{score},\mathit{eligible}},
\]
where $\mathit{score}$ is a function on blocks and $\mathit{eligible}$ a
predicate on blocks.  It descends from $\mathit{anchor}$ through eligible
children in $\mathit{tree}$, taking the highest score at each step, and stops
where no child is eligible.

Goldfish instantiates the walk with
$\fname{goldfish\_score}(\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s,\cdot)$
and the eligibility
condition
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\Call{goldfish\_eligible}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
   \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}\\
 &\quad\iff 2\,\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},
   \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}>\mathit{voters\_count}\text{ or }B.\slot=\Sigma.s,
\end{aligned}
\]
with $\mathit{voters\_count}=
|\{v:\mathit{votes}\text{ holds a vote by }v\}|$.
The majority condition can be used to enforce timeliness and other fork-choice conditions, like FOCIL inclusion. It only does not apply to
proposals from the current slot, which cannot yet have votes.
\end{definition}

At slot~$0$ the vote set is empty, no child is eligible, and the head is
genesis.

\captionof{figure}{Goldfish score and walk}\label{alg:goldfish}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{goldfish\_score}{$\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s,B$}
  \State $\mathit{equivocators}\gets\{v:
    \mathit{votes}\text{ holds two distinct votes by }v\}$
  \State $\mathit{supporters}\gets
    \{v\notin\mathit{equivocators}:
    (v,s,B')\in\mathit{support\_votes}\text{ with }B\preceq B'\}$
  \State \Return $|\mathit{equivocators}|+|\mathit{supporters}|$
  \Comment{equivocators count for every block}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{ghost}{$\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
  \mathit{score},\mathit{eligible}$}
  \State $H\gets\mathit{anchor}$
  \Loop
    \State $\mathit{children}\gets\{B:B\text{ is a child of }H\text{ in }
      \mathit{tree},\ \mathit{eligible}(B)\}$
    \If{$\mathit{children}=\emptyset$} \State \Return $H$ \EndIf
    \State $H\gets\arg\max_{B\in\mathit{children}}\mathit{score}(B)$,
      ties by root order
  \EndLoop
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_eligible}{$\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
  \mathit{support\_votes},s,B$}
  \State $\mathit{voters\_count}\gets
    |\{v:\mathit{votes}\text{ holds a vote by }v\}|$
  \State \Return $2\,\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}
    >\mathit{voters\_count}$ or $B.\slot=\Sigma.s$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_fork\_choice}{$\Sigma,\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
  \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s$}
  \State define $\mathit{score}(B)$ as
  \Statex \hspace{2em}
    $\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s,B}$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
  \Statex \hspace{2em}
    $\Call{goldfish\_eligible}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}$
  \State \Return $\Call{ghost}{\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
    \mathit{score},\mathit{eligible}}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_head}{$\Sigma,\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s$}
  \State \Return $\Call{goldfish\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,\genesis,\Sigma.\T,
    \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s}$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\subsection{Duties and handlers}\label{sec:goldfish-handlers}

Figure~\ref{alg:goldfish-store} defines the store handlers and the two duties.
The handlers take the store and change it in place.  Only the two duties
use the local validator index, written~$\valindex$.  It is an external input
to the node, not a field of $\Sigma$ or the validator record introduced
later.  A node with no duty for the slot does not run the duty.

\captionof{figure}{Goldfish duties and store handlers}
\label{alg:goldfish-store}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{on\_tick}{$\Sigma,t$}
  \State $s\gets\lfloor t/(4\Delta)\rfloor$; $(\Sigma.t,\Sigma.s)\gets(t,s)$
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s$ and $\valindex$ is the slot-$s$ proposer}
    \State \Call{propose\_block}{$\Sigma$}
  \EndIf
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s+\Delta$}
    \State \Call{goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma$}
  \EndIf
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s+2\Delta$}
    \State \Call{update\_confirmation}{$\Sigma,s-1$}
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_block}{$\Sigma,B$}
  \If{$B.\slot>\Sigma.s$ or $B\in\Sigma.\T$ or
      $B.\parent\notin\Sigma.\T$} \State \Return \EndIf
  \State add $B$ to $\Sigma.\T$; $\Sigma.\timestamp(B)\gets\Sigma.t$
  \ForAll{$\mathit{vote}\in B.\mathsf{votes}$}
    \State \Call{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \EndFor
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \If{$\mathit{vote}.\slot<\Sigma.s-1$ or $\mathit{vote}.\slot>\Sigma.s$ or
    $\mathit{vote}.\valindex\notin\committee_{\mathit{vote}.\slot}$ or
    $\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$}
    \State \Return \EndIf
  \If{$\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$ holds two distinct votes by
    $\mathit{vote}.\valindex$}
    \State \Return
    \Comment{two witness the equivocation; no rule needs a third pool entry}
  \EndIf
  \State add $\mathit{vote}$ to $\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$;
    $\Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})\gets\Sigma.t$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{propose\_block}{$\Sigma$}
  \State $s\gets\Sigma.s$
  \Comment{runs at $t_s$}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\Sigma.\gfPool[s-1]$
  \Comment{every previous-slot vote held}
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<+\infty\}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},s-1}$
  \State $B\gets$ a block with $B.\parent=H$, $B.\slot=s$,
    $B.\proposer=\valindex$
  \State $B.\mathsf{votes}\gets\mathit{votes}$
    \Comment{everything, not only the pre-freeze part}
  \State $B.\mathsf{support\_votes}\gets\mathit{support\_votes}$
    \Comment{the proposal-time support subset}
  \State broadcast $B$; \Call{on\_block}{$\Sigma,B$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma$}
  \State $s\gets\Sigma.s$
  \Comment{runs at $t_s+\Delta$}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s-1]:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})<t_{s-1}+3\Delta\}$
    \Comment{received before the freeze}
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<t_{s-1}+3\Delta\}$
  \ForAll{$B\in\Sigma.\T$ with $B.\slot=s$}
    \State $\mathit{included\_votes}\gets
      \{\mathit{vote}\in B.\mathsf{votes}:\mathit{vote}.\slot=s-1\}$
    \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\mathit{votes}\cup\mathit{included\_votes}$
    \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\mathit{support\_votes}\cup
      \{(v,s-1,H)\in B.\mathsf{support\_votes}:H\in\Sigma.\T\}$
    \Comment{fixed proposal support}
  \EndFor
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},s-1}$
  \If{$\valindex\in\committee_s$}
    \State $\mathit{vote}\gets(\valindex,s,H)$; broadcast $\mathit{vote}$;
      \Call{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\subsection{Available confirmation}\label{sec:available-confirmation}

Confirmation is the same walk over a stricter vote set and a larger
denominator.  Let $\mathit{early\_votes}$ be the slot-$s$ votes with
resolution times before $t_s+2\Delta$, and $\mathit{late\_votes}$ the slot-$s$ votes
timestamped before $t_s+6\Delta$, whatever their resolution.  The walk scores
\[
 \mathit{support\_votes}=\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{early\_votes}:
  \mathit{vote}.\valindex\text{ does not equivocate in }
  \mathit{late\_votes}\}
\]
against the participant count of $\mathit{late\_votes}$.  A validator counts when it
voted in time and no second vote of its has appeared since; the denominator
counts everyone who voted at all.

Because $\mathit{early\_votes}\subseteq\mathit{late\_votes}$, a validator
equivocating in $\mathit{early\_votes}$ equivocates in
$\mathit{late\_votes}$ too, so $\mathit{support\_votes}$
holds at most one vote per validator and the equivocator clause of the score
never fires here.  At most one child can hold a majority, so the descent
has no choice to make.

Slot~$s$ is evaluated once, at $t_s+6\Delta$, from genesis over the
processed block tree.
The result is at worst genesis, never empty.

\noindent\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
\captionof{figure}{Available confirmation}\label{alg:confirmation}
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{update\_confirmation}{$\Sigma,s$}
  \Comment{at $t_s+6\Delta$}
  \State $\mathit{early\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s]:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<t_s+2\Delta\}$
  \State $\mathit{late\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s]:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})<t_s+6\Delta\}$
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets
    \{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{early\_votes}:
    \mathit{late\_votes}\text{ holds no second distinct vote by }
    \mathit{vote}.\valindex\}$
  \State $\mathit{voters\_count}\gets
    |\{v:\mathit{late\_votes}\text{ holds a vote by }v\}|$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
    $2\,\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{support\_votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}>\mathit{voters\_count}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{ghost}{\genesis,\Sigma.\T,
    \fname{goldfish\_score}(\mathit{support\_votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,\cdot),\mathit{eligible}}$
  \State $\Sigma.\liveConfirmed\gets H$
  \If{$\Sigma.\latestConfirmed\preceq H$}
    \State $\Sigma.\latestConfirmed\gets H$
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}
\end{minipage}

\section{Relative-majority stabilization gadget}\label{sec:majority-sg}

SG means \emph{stabilization gadget}.  This intermediate protocol adds one
message layer to Goldfish: a relative-majority fork choice over the latest SG
votes selects the root from which the Goldfish walk starts.

\subsection{Rounds and SG votes}\label{sec:sg-schedule}

Each validator $v\in V$ has a fixed positive integer weight~$w(v)$.  For
$S\subseteq V$, write $w(S)=\sum_{v\in S}w(v)$ and $W=w(V)$.

For a fixed integer~$R\geq1$, round~$r$ consists of slots $rR,\ldots,rR+R-1$;
slot~$rR$ is its \emph{opening slot}, and $\round(s)=\lfloor s/R\rfloor$.
Each round has one SG vote time~$a_r$, a public parameter in this
intermediate protocol.

An SG vote is a tuple $(\valindex,r,C)$ from validator $\valindex\in V$ with confirmed
block~$C$, a block or~$\bot$.  At~$a_r$, an honest validator votes its current
$\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$, which is a block; the empty value appears only in
adversarial votes.  A
round-$r$ vote is read from round~$r+1$ on.

\subsection{Store}\label{sec:sg-store}

This layer adds one field: $\Sigma.\attestations[r]$, which at this layer
holds the processed round-$r$ SG votes.  Like $\Sigma.\gfPool$, it is
timestamped and keeps at most two distinct
votes per validator, which is all any rule reads; two distinct confirmed
blocks from one validator in one round are an equivocation.  For an SG vote
with confirmed block~$C$, the derived function is
\[
 \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})=
 \begin{cases}
  \Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote}), & C=\bot,\\
  \max\{\Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote}),\Sigma.\timestamp(C)\},
    & C\in\Sigma.\T,\\
  +\infty, & C\neq\bot\text{ and }C\notin\Sigma.\T.
 \end{cases}
\]
Thus, an SG vote is resolved when the store holds its nonempty confirmed
block, and an empty confirmed block resolves at receipt.  SG votes travel only on the
wire.  Blocks do not carry them, and they never enter a Goldfish vote set.

An honest node forwards every accepted SG vote while its round is in the
$\eta_{\rm SG}$ expiry window.  The same rule later applies to combined
attestations.  Messages outside this fixed round window need not be
accepted or forwarded.  Old finality pairs need not remain available on
the wire because validators can submit target and timeout votes again.
Expired pool entries may be discarded.

\subsection{Latest votes and majority root}\label{sec:sg-fork-choice}

\begin{definition}[Latest support vote and majority root]
\label{def:majority-fork-choice}
Fix an expiry window $\eta_{\rm SG}\geq1$ in rounds.  For validator~$v$ and
round~$r$, consider the greatest round in
$[\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\},\,r)$ in which $\Sigma.\attestations[k]$ holds a resolved
vote by~$v$.  $\Call{latest\_support\_vote}{\Sigma,v,r}$ returns the unique
such SG vote, or~$\bot$ if no such round exists or that
round holds more than one distinct SG vote by~$v$.  Thus, equivocation is
is detected even when one of the votes in this round is
unresolved: receiving two votes is enough. Note that, since we consider the greatest round with a resolved vote, merely receiving a vote at a newer round newer does not replace the current support vote, until the vote is resolved. This is intentional, to prevent asynchrony from ever reducing honest support weight.
\\
A validator is \emph{represented} when the expiry window holds any of its
votes, resolved or not.  It \emph{supports} a block~$B$ when
$\Call{latest\_support\_vote}{\Sigma,v,r}=(v,k,C)$ with $C\neq\bot$ and
$B\preceq C$.

$\Call{sg\_support}{\Sigma,r,B}$ is the weight supporting~$B$.
The entire represented weight is
$W_r=w(\{v\in V:\exists k\in[\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\},r),\
  \Sigma.\attestations[k]\text{ holds a vote by }v\})$.
Ancestry is read in the processed block tree~$\Sigma.\T$, so a confirmed block outside a restricted child
tree still supports the child through which it descends.
$\Call{majority\_fork\_choice}{}$ runs the walk with this score, requiring
from each child a strict majority of~$W_r$.  Because a support-round
equivocator supplies no support but stays in the denominator, and
conflicting children have disjoint supporter
sets, two conflicting children cannot both pass: the descent is uniquely
determined, and equivocating weight can only raise the bar.
\end{definition}

The composed head runs Goldfish from the majority root instead of genesis
(Figure~\ref{alg:sg-fork-choice}): the SG walk selects the anchor, and the
Goldfish walk selects a descendant of it.  The two walks are the same
function with different scores and eligibility conditions.

\captionof{figure}{SG fork choice}\label{alg:sg-fork-choice}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{latest\_support\_vote}{$\Sigma,v,r$}
  \State $\mathit{eligible}\gets\{r':\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\}\leq r'<r,\
    \exists a\in\Sigma.\attestations[r'],\ a.\valindex=v,\
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(a)<+\infty\}$
  \If{$\mathit{eligible}=\emptyset$} \State \Return $\bot$ \EndIf
  \State $r^*\gets\max\mathit{eligible}$
  \If{$\Sigma.\attestations[r^*]$ does not hold exactly one distinct SG vote by~$v$}
    \State \Return $\bot$
  \EndIf
  \State \Return that entry's SG-vote projection $(v,r^*,C)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{sg\_support}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \State $\mathit{supporters}\gets\emptyset$
  \ForAll{$v\in V$}
    \State $\mathit{vote}\gets\Call{latest\_support\_vote}{\Sigma,v,r}$
    \If{$\mathit{vote}=(v,r^*,C)$ with $C\neq\bot$ and $B\preceq C$}
      \State $\mathit{supporters}\gets\mathit{supporters}\cup\{v\}$
    \EndIf
  \EndFor
  \State \Return $w(\mathit{supporters})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{majority\_fork\_choice}{$\Sigma,\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},r$}
  \State $\mathit{total}\gets
    w(\{v\in V:\exists r':\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\}\leq r'<r,\
      \Sigma.\attestations[r']\text{ holds a vote by }v\})$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
    $2\,\Call{sg\_support}{\Sigma,r,B}>\mathit{total}$
  \State \Return $\Call{ghost}{\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
    \fname{sg\_support}(\Sigma,r,\cdot),\mathit{eligible}}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_head}{$\Sigma,\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s$}
  \State $\mathit{anchor}\gets\Call{majority\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,\genesis,
    \Sigma.\T,\round(\Sigma.s)}$
  \State \Return $\Call{goldfish\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,\mathit{anchor},
    \Sigma.\T,\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s}$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\section{Finality-gadget state transition}\label{sec:state-machine}

The finality gadget extends each non-genesis block with a list of combined
attestations.  It also defines the
weighted quorum threshold
\[
 q=\left\lceil\frac{2W}{3}\right\rceil.
\]
A quorum is a set of validators whose total weight is at least~$q$.

\subsection{Combined attestations}

Height is a finality counter and is separate from slot.  Genesis is justified
and finalized at height~$0$, and every chain starts at height~$1$.  A
justification or a progress event increments height by one.

We extend the attestation to
\[
 a=(\valindex,\round,\confirmedf,
     \height,\target,\mathsf{finalize\_height},
     \mathsf{finalize\_target}),
\]
adding the finality-relevant fields $\height$,
$\target, \mathsf{finalize\_height}, \mathsf{finalize\_target}$ to the SG vote. The height pair is a target vote $(h,T)$ with $T\neq\bot$, an empty-target vote
$(h,\bot)$, or the empty pair $(\bot,\bot)$.  The finality pair is
$(h_f,T_f)$ with $T_f\neq\bot$, or the empty pair $(\bot,\bot)$.
 

Two pair occurrences from one validator are slashable under either
condition:
\begin{description}
 \item[E1:] one finality pair is $(h,T)$ with $T\neq\bot$, and one height
   pair is $(h,T')$ with $T'\neq T$, including $T'=\bot$;
 \item[E2:] two height pairs are $(h,T)$ and $(h,T')$ with
   $T,T'\neq\bot$ and $T\neq T'$.
\end{description}
The conflicting occurrences can be in one attestation.  An empty height pair
has height~$\bot$ and triggers neither condition.

An honest validator emits at most one proposal per slot, one Goldfish vote
per slot, and one combined attestation per round.

\subsection{Chain state and transition}

Every block is evaluated from its parent's immutable post-state:
\[
 \sigma[B]=\Call{state\_transition}{\sigma[B.\parent],B}.
\]
The result is a deterministic function of the chain ending at~$B$.

\begin{definition}[Chain state]\label{def:chain-state}
A chain state is
\[
 \sigma=(L,s,h,T_h,\nj,
  \targetParticipation,\progressBit,\finalityBit,
  J,h_j,F,h_F).
\]
$\sigma.L$ is the latest block, $\sigma.s=\sigma.L.\slot$, $\sigma.h$ is the
current height, and $\sigma.T_h$ is the block that carried the transition
into height~$\sigma.h$.  The Boolean $\sigma.\nj$ is computed when the chain
enters~$\sigma.h$ and remains fixed until the height
changes.

The arrays $\sigma.\targetParticipation$, $\sigma.\progressBit$, and
$\sigma.\finalityBit$ contain
one Boolean value per validator.  Their quorum sets are derived when used:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  \Qtarget(\sigma)&=\{i:\sigma.\targetParticipation[i]\},&
  \Qprog(\sigma)&=\{i:\sigma.\progressBit[i]\},\\
  \Qfin(\sigma)&=\{i:\sigma.\finalityBit[i]\}.&&
 \end{aligned}
\]
The pair $(\sigma.J,\sigma.h_j)$ is the latest justification on the chain,
and $(\sigma.F,\sigma.h_F)$ is its latest finalization.  Initially
\[
 \sigma.L=\sigma.T_h=\sigma.J=\sigma.F=\genesis,\quad
 \sigma.s=0,\quad \sigma.h=1,\quad \sigma.h_j=\sigma.h_F=0,
 \quad\sigma.\nj=\mathsf{false},
\]
and every array entry is false.
\end{definition}

Fix constants $K\geq3$, $D\geq2$ and a timeout delay $\delta_{\mathrm{t}}\geq0$ in slots; $K$ is meant large, $D$ minimal, and \S\ref{sec:healing} sets $\delta_{\mathrm{t}}=2R$, two rounds. Timeout rows are recorded in $\progressBit$ as soon as they are folded, but a progress quorum that needs them is consumed only by a block whose slot is at least $\sigma.T_h.\slot+\delta_{\mathrm{t}}$, where $\sigma.T_h$ is the block that entered the height; an exact target quorum is consumed immediately: attestations are synchronous at the scale of rounds, so a timeout may not be used to leave a height before the target votes cast at that height have had time to arrive. Without the delay, faulty timeouts released right after an honest block that folded a partial honest target batch can cross the height without justifying it.
The steady pipeline enters heights at debt $\sigma.h-\sigma.h_F=2$, so $D\geq2$;
$K\geq3$ avoids a skip cadence that regenerates itself at $K=2$.
On entry into a new height~$\sigma.h$, after
the same transition has applied any finalization, set
\[
 \sigma.\nj\gets(K\mid\sigma.h)\wedge(\sigma.h-\sigma.h_F>D).
\]

\section{Finality gadget in the fork-choice}\label{sec:fg-fork-choice}

\subsection{Stored states and finality updates}

The store adds a block state map $\Sigma.\sigma[\cdot]$ and finality state
$(\Sigma.F,\Sigma.J,\Sigma.h_j,\Sigma.\hmax)$.
Figure~\ref{alg:fg-store} extends the block handler: it admits only
descendants of the finalized block, computes and stores
the post-state, then folds it into the finality caches with
$\Call{update\_finality}{\Sigma,\sigma}$.  The pair
$(\Sigma.J,\Sigma.h_j)$ tracks the lex-greatest justification event
compatible with the finalized block.  $\Sigma.F$ advances
only to a viable proper descendant of itself below~$\Sigma.J$, so
$\Sigma.F\preceq\Sigma.J$ always holds and finalization never reverts.
$\Sigma.\hmax$ only grows, and every block that raises it descends the
finalized block at that moment.  When $\Sigma.F$ advances, an admitted
block can stop descending it and its branch freezes: its children now
fail admission.  The viability guard on the advance then keeps
$\Sigma.\hmax$ at most one above the largest state height among the
finalized block's descendants, so $\Sigma.F$ itself is always viable.
The figure follows the viability definitions it uses.

\subsection{Fork choice}

\begin{definition}[Viability]
\label{def:fg-candidate-tree}
A descendant of the finalized block is viable when one of its
descendants has state height at most one below the current maximum.
Figure~\ref{alg:fg-store} defines
$\Call{viable\_tree\_from}{\Sigma,\mathit{blocks}}$ over an explicit
processed-block view.  The ordinary
$\Call{viable\_tree}{\Sigma}$ uses $\Sigma.\T$, and in prose
$\viableTree(\Sigma)$ abbreviates it.
\end{definition}

Fork choice uses two derived views (Figure~\ref{alg:fg-store}):
$\Call{get\_fg\_root}{\Sigma}$, the FG root --- the block the walk
starts from --- and
$\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree\_from}{\Sigma,\mathit{blocks}}$,
the viable blocks below it in an explicit processed-block view.
$\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$ uses the full
$\Sigma.\T$.  Goldfish starts at the root even if the root is not in
the filtered tree.

At this layer $\Call{goldfish\_eligible}{}$ gains one clause: a child
of a block whose state height is below $\Sigma.\hmax-1$ is eligible
without a majority.  The majority gate thus applies exactly while the walk
stands inside the band $\{\Sigma.\hmax-1,\Sigma.\hmax\}$: every viable
block below the band has a viable child, so the walk always reaches
height $\Sigma.\hmax-1$, and it never passes through the band without a
majority.  Figure~\ref{alg:fg-store} shows the extended store handler, the
extended eligibility condition, and the redefined $\Call{get\_head}{}$.  Available confirmation is unchanged at
this layer; the complete protocol re-anchors it at the SG root over the
filtered block tree, with the FG root as the fallback value.

\EndFunction

\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}

\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{$\Sigma$}

\subsection{SG/FG attestation}

\begin{definition}[Anti-slashing record]\label{def:antislashing}
The record $\Lambda$ holds, for every height~$h$, the validator's first
target $\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h]$, whether it emitted an empty-target
vote $\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h]$, and the target of its first finality
pair $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h]$; initially
$(\bot,\mathsf{false},\bot)$.  It belongs to the validator, not to the store,
and $\Call{record\_attestation}{}$ is the only place that writes it. A validator that never voted at height~$h_j$ may still emit a finality pair for $(h_j,J)$: the pair writes $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_j]\gets J$ before release, so from then on its height pair at $h_j$ is either $(h_j,J)$ or empty, never a timeout or another target; only a recorded timeout or a different first target at $h_j$ refuses the pair.  Together,
$\Sigma$ and $\Lambda$ determine the combined attestation.  This is modeled
after the anti-slashing database of today's validators: a minimal, hardened
component of the validator client, separate from the beacon client that
keeps~$\Sigma$.  $\Call{create\_attestation}{}$ is its whole interface:
it takes the record and the vote information, produces the attestation,
and updates the record before releasing it; it never reads the store.
\end{definition}

The height pair is built from a confirmed block's height fields: a target
vote can create a justification, and
only confirmed blocks may be justified.  The finality pair is built from
the head's finality fields: its subject is
already justified, so it may read an unconfirmed chain, and the head
chain state carries the freshest justification.  Here $C$ is
$\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$, or the relative-majority anchor when the anchor
does not precede it: the vote never falls behind or beside the
represented chain.  $H$ is the fork-choice head; the
graded action passes its own confirmed block and head.
$\Call{attest}{\Sigma,\Lambda}$ is the beacon-client side: it derives
the confirmed block, the head, and their chain-state fields from the
store, and
broadcasts what $\Call{create\_attestation}{}$ releases.  The pair rules
only read the record; $\Call{record\_attestation}{\Lambda,a}$ folds the
emitted attestation into it before release, and
$\Call{height\_pair}{}$ receives the same attestation's finality pair as
the lock at its height, so one attestation never forms E1 evidence.
$\Call{on\_tick}{}$ gains the record argument and, at~$a_r$, runs
$\Call{attest}{}$ in place of $\Call{sg\_vote}{}$.

\captionof{figure}{FG attestation}\label{alg:pair-rules}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{finality\_pair}{$\Lambda,h_j,J,h_F$}
  \If{$h_j>h_F$}
    \Comment{a justification not yet finalized}
    \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_j]\in\{\bot,J\}$ and
        not $\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h_j]$ and
        $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_j]\in\{\bot,J\}$}
      \Comment{the record allows it}
      \State \Return $(h_j,J)$
    \EndIf
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{height\_pair}{$\Lambda,h_c,T_c,\nu,h_f,T_f$}
  \If{$h_c=\bot$} \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$ \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{lock}\gets\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_c]$;\quad
    \textbf{if} $h_c=h_f$ \textbf{then} $\mathit{lock}\gets T_f$
  \Comment{this attestation's own lock}
  \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h_c]$}
    \State \Return $(h_c,\bot)$
    \Comment{a timeout repeats}
  \EndIf
  \If{$\mathit{lock}\neq\bot$}
    \Comment{a lock repeats}
    \If{$\mathit{lock}=T_c$}
      \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \EndIf
    \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$
    \Comment{locked elsewhere: wait out the height}
  \EndIf
  \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_c]\neq\bot$}
    \Comment{a recorded target repeats}
    \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_c]=T_c$}
      \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \EndIf
  \ElsIf{not $\nu$}
    \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \Comment{no history: adopt the chain's target}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $(h_c,\bot)$
  \Comment{otherwise time out at $h_c$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{create\_attestation}{$\Lambda,r,\mathit{confirmed},
  h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F$}
  \Comment{validator-client side: no store access}
  \State $(h_f,T_f)\gets\Call{finality\_pair}{\Lambda,h_j,J,h_F}$
  \State $(h,T)\gets\Call{height\_pair}{\Lambda,h_c,T_c,\nu,h_f,T_f}$
  \State $a\gets(\valindex,r,\mathit{confirmed},h,T,h_f,T_f)$
  \State \Call{record\_attestation}{$\Lambda,a$}; \Return $a$
  \Comment{record, then release}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{record\_attestation}{$\Lambda,a$}
  \Comment{the only writer of $\Lambda$}
  \State $(h,T,h_f,T_f)\gets a$'s height and finality pairs
  \If{$T_f\neq\bot$} \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_f]\gets T_f$ \EndIf
  \If{$h\neq\bot$}
    \If{$T\neq\bot$ and $\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h]=\bot$}
      \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h]\gets T$
    \EndIf
    \If{$T=\bot$}
      \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h]\gets\mathsf{true}$
    \EndIf
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{attest}{$\Sigma,\Lambda$}
  \State $r\gets\round(\Sigma.s)$;\quad $C\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$
  \Comment{runs at $a_r$}
  \State $A\gets\Call{get\_sg\_root}{\Sigma,r}$;\quad
    \textbf{if} $A\not\preceq C$ \textbf{then} $C\gets A$
  \Comment{never vote behind the SG root}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\Sigma.\gfPool[\Sigma.s]$
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<+\infty\}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},\Sigma.s}$
  \Comment{current-slot votes have arrived by $a_r$}
  \State $(h_c,T_c,\nu)\gets(\Sigma.\sigma[C].h,\Sigma.\sigma[C].T_h,
    \Sigma.\sigma[C].\nj)$
  \State $(h_j,J,h_F)\gets(\Sigma.\sigma[H].h_j,\Sigma.\sigma[H].J,
    \Sigma.\sigma[H].h_F)$
  \State $a\gets\Call{create\_attestation}{\Lambda,r,C,h_c,T_c,\nu,
    h_j,J,h_F}$
  \State broadcast $a$;
    \Call{on\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,(\valindex,r,C)$}
  \Comment{the SG-vote projection}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_tick}{$\Sigma,\Lambda,t$}
  \State $s\gets\lfloor t/(4\Delta)\rfloor$; $(\Sigma.t,\Sigma.s)\gets(t,s)$
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s$ and $\valindex$ is the slot-$s$ proposer}
    \State \Call{propose\_block}{$\Sigma$}
  \EndIf
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s+\Delta$}
    \State \Call{goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma$}
  \EndIf
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s+2\Delta$}
    \State \Call{update\_confirmation}{$\Sigma,s-1$}
  \EndIf
  \If{$t=a_r$ for the current round $r$}
    \State \Call{attest}{$\Sigma,\Lambda$}
    \Comment{in place of $\Call{sg\_vote}{}$}
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\section{Graded SG healing}\label{sec:healing}

Healing gives a block with fresh absolute-majority support precedence
over the relative-majority anchor, where fresh means votes of the
previous round only, and makes the finality-gadget input conditional on
fresh support.  Round~$r$ grades blocks by the support of round-$(r-1)$
SG votes at four cutoffs around the opening slot.  Grade~2 enables the
height pair and serves as its fallback source; grade~1 anchors the fork
choice; grade~0 vetoes attesting to conflicting blocks.  There is no
proposal mechanism at this layer.  When no block holds a grade from
round~$r-1$, the fork choice and the SG vote reduce to the protocol of
Sections~\ref{sec:goldfish}--\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, and the
attestation carries an empty height pair.

\subsection{Round schedule}\label{sec:healing-schedule}

Assume $R\geq2$ slots per round, and set the timeout delay of \S\ref{sec:state-machine} to $\delta_{\mathrm{t}}=2R$: a timeout at a height is counted only from the second round after the round in which the height was entered, because target votes of one round are assumed to reach every honest store by the end of the next round.  The four grade cutoffs bracket the
opening slot's proposal and vote times:
\[
 \Gamma_r^{-1}=t_{rR}-\Delta,\qquad
 \Gamma_r^0=t_{rR},\qquad
 \Gamma_r^1=t_{rR}+\Delta,\qquad
 \Gamma_r^2=t_{rR}+2\Delta.
\]
The action time is $a_r=t_{rR}+6\Delta$, the opening slot's confirmation
evaluation: $\Call{on\_tick}{}$ evaluates that confirmation and then runs
$\Call{attest}{}$ in the same tick.

\subsection{Grades}\label{sec:grades}

\begin{definition}[Grade supports and grades]\label{def:grades}
The absolute-majority threshold is $m=\lfloor W/2\rfloor+1$.  Round~$r$
grades read only the fresh SG-vote projections in
$\Sigma.\attestations[r-1]$.  For validator~$v$,
$\Call{grade\_summary}{\Sigma,r,v}$ derives $(C_v,t_v,e_v)$.  Here, $C_v$
is the confirmed block of $v$'s first resolved vote naming one, and $t_v$
is that resolution time, or $(\bot,+\infty)$ when there is no such vote.
Equal resolution times use the fixed root order.  The value $e_v$ is the
time at which the pool first holds two of $v$'s votes with distinct
confirmed blocks, an empty value included --- the receipt timestamp of the
second --- or~$+\infty$ when it never does.
Direct support deletes equivocators, as $\fname{sg\_support}$ does;
favorable support credits them, as $\fname{goldfish\_score}$ does;
Figure~\ref{alg:grades} defines both supports and the three grades.
In prose, $\Gtwo(B)$ abbreviates
$\Call{G2}{\Sigma,r,B}$ with the store and round from context, and
likewise grades 1 and~0; pseudocode spells the call.
Each grade is eligible only in its own round; round~$0$ has no fresh
attestations and no grades.
\end{definition}

In one store, conflicting blocks cannot both hold a direct grade because
the first-resolved vote~$C_v$ is a single block and $2m>W$.
Thus, the direct-grade blocks in one store lie on one chain, and the
deepest grade-1 and grade-2 selections below are well defined.  In one
validator's store, each grade implies the next,
\mbox{$\Gtwo(B)\implies\Gone(B)\implies\Gzero(B)$}: a grade-2 supporter has
$t_v<\Gamma_r^{-1}<\Gamma_r^0$ and $e_v\geq\Gamma_r^2>\Gamma_r^0$, and a
grade-1 supporter satisfies the first favorable arm.

Across validators, \emph{graded delivery} is conditional on admission.
Under synchrony, a vote or evidence item counted at one cutoff reaches
every validator by the next, and a supporting vote resolves at the
receiver once the receiver admits its head, that is, once that head
extends the receiver's finalized block.  A grade at one honest validator
therefore implies the next-lower grade at every honest validator that
admits the supporting heads; a store that refuses them has finalized a
block conflicting with those heads, so the graded block lies below that
finalized block or conflicts with it.
From grade~2 here to grade~1 there, the supporting vote reaches the other
store before~$\Gamma_r^0$; a different first vote, or equivocation visible
there before~$\Gamma_r^0$, reaches this store before~$\Gamma_r^2$ and would
sweep that supporter from grade~2.  From grade~1 there to grade~0 here,
each supporter either counts in the first favorable arm or supplies
$e_v<\Gamma_r^1$; the graded block itself, an ancestor of a resolved head,
is processed here before~$\Gamma_r^1$.

Under the same conditions, grade-2 blocks across honest stores lie on one
chain.  A grade-2 block is also never vetoed in any honest store: a
conflicting favorable majority before $\Gamma_r^1$ and a direct majority
clean through~$\Gamma_r^2$ would share a validator, since $2m>W$, whose
conflicting vote or evidence reaches the grade-2 store by
$\Gamma_r^1+\Delta=\Gamma_r^2$.

The protocol uses each grade only after its defining cutoffs have closed:
grade~1 from~$\Gamma_r^0$, grade~0 from~$\Gamma_r^1$, and grade~2
from~$\Gamma_r^2$.

A grade is \emph{active} when its block is in the filtered block tree of
Section~\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}: it descends the FG root and is
viable, so only blocks the fork choice can use carry active grades.
Grades~2 and~1 are used positively, as a source and as an anchor, and
only when active.  Grade~0 is used only negatively, as a veto, and it is
read on every processed block: a fresh favorable majority vetoes the
blocks its block conflicts with even when that block has left the band
or the root has passed it.  Such a majority shows that the previous round
did not settle on the confirmed block, whatever the position of its own
block.  The grade-0 veto reads only blocks processed before the
freeze~$\Gamma_r^1$: a block learned after the freeze never becomes a
veto.

\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{grade\_summary}{$\Sigma,r,v$}
  \If{$r=0$} \State \Return $(\bot,+\infty,+\infty)$ \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{fresh}\gets
    \{a\in\Sigma.\attestations[r-1]:
    a.\valindex=v\}$
  \State $\mathit{resolved}\gets\{a\in\mathit{fresh}:
    a.\confirmedf\neq\bot,\ \Sigma.\resolutionTime(a)<+\infty\}$
  \If{$\mathit{resolved}=\emptyset$}
    \State $(C_v,t_v)\gets(\bot,+\infty)$
  \Else
    \State $(t_v,C_v)\gets
      \min\{(\Sigma.\resolutionTime(a),a.\confirmedf):
      a\in\mathit{resolved}\}$
    \Comment{lexicographic; root order breaks ties}
  \EndIf
  \State $e_v\gets\max\{\Sigma.\timestamp(a):
    a\in\mathit{fresh}\}$ if
  $|\mathit{fresh}|>1$, else $+\infty$
  \State \Return $(C_v,t_v,e_v)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{direct\_support}{$\Sigma,r,\Gamma_h,\Gamma_e,B$}
  \State for each $v\in V$, let
    $(C_v,t_v,e_v)\gets\Call{grade\_summary}{\Sigma,r,v}$
  \State \Return $w(\{v\in V:t_v<\Gamma_h,\ B\preceq C_v,\
    e_v\geq\Gamma_e\})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{favorable\_support}{$\Sigma,r,\Gamma,B$}
  \State for each $v\in V$, let
    $(C_v,t_v,e_v)\gets\Call{grade\_summary}{\Sigma,r,v}$
  \State \Return $w(\{v\in V:t_v<\Gamma,\ B\preceq C_v\}
    \cup\{v\in V:e_v<\Gamma\})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G2}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \State \Return $\Call{direct\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^{-1},
    \Gamma_r^2,B}\geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G1}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \State \Return $\Call{direct\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^0,
    \Gamma_r^0,B}\geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G0}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \State \Return $\Call{favorable\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^1,B}
    \geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G0\_conflict\_free}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \State $\mathit{vetoes}\gets
    \{B'\in\Sigma.T:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(B')<\Gamma_r^1,$
  \Statex \hspace{4.35em} $B'\text{ conflicts with }B,\
    \Call{G0}{\Sigma,r,B'}\}$
  \Comment{every processed block, active or not}
  \State \Return $\mathit{vetoes}=\emptyset$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\subsection{The SG root}\label{sec:fresh-anchor}

The SG root is the \emph{fresh SG root} when one exists --- the deepest
block of the filtered tree with grade~1 ---, the fork-choice root when
some block holds grade~1 but none of them is in the filtered tree, and the
relative-majority anchor otherwise, that is, only when no block holds
grade~1 at all; in walk position the SG root is the anchor, and the prose
calls it that.  The middle case is a fresh finalization: the fork-choice
root has advanced past every grade-1 block, and the root itself is then
canonical, so it is the anchor.  The relative-majority walk is thus
reserved for rounds without an absolute quorum.  The selection is derived
at every use, from the current store; nothing is stored.  For each fixed
block, its grade-1 predicate is fixed from~$\Gamma_r^0$ through the rest
of round~$r$.
Conflicting blocks cannot both hold grade~1 in one store, so ``deepest''
is well defined.  A fresh SG root in use at an honest validator is a
grade-1 block of its store.  Under the graded-delivery
conditions above, by~$\Gamma_r^1$ it holds grade~0, timestamped before
the freeze, in every honest store that admits its supporting heads, and
it is a veto there whether or not that store's filtered tree contains it;
a store that refuses those heads has finalized a block they conflict
with.

\captionof{figure}{The SG root}\label{alg:fresh-anchor}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{get\_sg\_root}{$\Sigma,r$}
  \Comment{redefines the \S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice} body}
  \State $\mathit{tree}\gets\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$
  \If{some $B\in\mathit{tree}$ and
      $\Call{G1}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return the deepest such $B$
    \Comment{the fresh SG root}
  \ElsIf{some $B\in\Sigma.T$ and
      $\Call{G1}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return $\fname{get\_fg\_root}(\Sigma)$
    \Comment{grade~1 exists, but the root has passed it}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $\Call{majority\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,
    \fname{get\_fg\_root}(\Sigma),\mathit{tree},r}$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\subsection{The round action}\label{sec:healing-action}

$\Call{attest}{}$ builds the SG vote~$\Csg$, computed by
$\Call{get\_sg\_vote}{}$, and the finality-gadget fields, computed by
$\Call{get\_fg\_vote}{}$ from the height-pair source~$\Cfg$ and the
head; $\Agtwo$ is the deepest active grade-2 block.  The SG vote is the deepest
veto-free block between the anchor and the confirmed block --- the
confirmed block itself whenever no grade-0 block conflicts.  When that
interval is empty the vote falls back to~$\Agtwo$, even though
it sits at or behind the anchor: the grade-2 block stays canonical ---
under the graded-delivery conditions above, every honest anchor whose
filtered tree holds it is at least as
deep --- while the anchor alone has no such guarantee.  When a grade-2
block exists but none is active --- the root has passed every grade-2
block, or conflicts them --- the vote is the FG root itself, which still
covers every grade-2 block below it; an absolute quorum is present, so
the untested anchor is never used.  The anchor is the last resort,
reserved for rounds with no grade-2 block at all, and only it keeps
voting total under dynamic participation; the veto operates only above
it, and none of the fallbacks takes a clearance test.  The height pair is enabled only when
$\Agtwo$ exists; its source is the deepest veto-free block between
$\Agtwo$ and the confirmed block, or $\Agtwo$ itself when that
walk is empty.  Starting there loses nothing: a block that
conflicts~$B$ conflicts every descendant of~$B$, and no grade-0 block
conflicts the grade-2 block or its ancestors.  Under the same conditions,
grade~2 here places grade~1 at every honest validator whose filtered tree
contains it.  An
anchor with grade~1 at an honest store holds grade~0 here, timestamped
before the freeze, whenever this store admits its supporting heads; it is
then a veto, in or out of this store's filtered tree, and the height-pair
source conflicts with no such anchor.  Only a store that has finalized a
block conflicting with those heads lacks that veto.  An empty confirmed value appears only
in adversarial votes: it represents the validator and silences its older
votes.

\captionof{figure}{Round action}\label{alg:round-action}
\nopagebreak
{\footnotesize
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{get\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,r,\Agtwo$}
  \State $C\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$;\quad
    $A\gets\Call{get\_sg\_root}{\Sigma,r}$
  \If{some $B$ with $A\preceq B\preceq C$ satisfies
      $\Call{G0\_conflict\_free}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return the deepest such $B$
  \EndIf
  \If{$\Agtwo\neq\bot$}
    \State \Return $\Agtwo$
    \Comment{the grade-2 block stays canonical}
  \ElsIf{some $B\in\Sigma.T$ and $\Call{G2}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return $\Call{get\_fg\_root}{\Sigma}$
    \Comment{grade~2 exists, but the root has passed it}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $A$
  \Comment{the anchor fallback keeps voting total}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_fg\_vote}{$\Sigma,r,\Agtwo$}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\Sigma.\gfPool[\Sigma.s]$
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<+\infty\}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},\Sigma.s}$
  \State $(h_j,J,h_F)\gets(\Sigma.\sigma[H].h_j,\Sigma.\sigma[H].J,
    \Sigma.\sigma[H].h_F)$
  \If{$\Agtwo=\bot$}
    \State \Return $(\bot,\bot,\bot,h_j,J,h_F)$
    \Comment{no fresh quorum: no height pair}
  \EndIf
  \State $C\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$
  \State $\Cfg\gets$ the deepest $B$ with $\Agtwo\preceq B\preceq C$
    and $\Call{G0\_conflict\_free}{\Sigma,r,B}$, else $\Agtwo$
  \Comment{the veto-free walk value, or the grade-2 block}
  \State \Return $(\Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].h,\Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].T_h,
    \Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].\nj,h_j,J,h_F)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{attest}{$\Sigma,\Lambda$}
  \State $r\gets\round(\Sigma.s)$
  \Comment{runs at $a_r$}
  \State $\Agtwo\gets$ the deepest
    $B\in\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$ with
    $\Call{G2}{\Sigma,r,B}$,
    else $\bot$
  \State $\Csg\gets\Call{get\_sg\_vote}{\Sigma,r,\Agtwo}$
  \State $(h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F)\gets
    \Call{get\_fg\_vote}{\Sigma,r,\Agtwo}$
  \State $a\gets\Call{create\_attestation}{\Lambda,r,\Csg,
    h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F}$
  \State broadcast $a$; \Call{on\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,a$}
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\subsection{Nonjustifiable heights}\label{sec:nonjustifiable}

The flag $\sigma.\nj$ of Section~\ref{sec:state-machine} disables justification
at every $K$-th height under finality debt, while exact and empty-target
votes still advance the height.  Healing therefore never waits on a
justification that the debt rule forbids.

\section{Complete protocol}\label{sec:complete-protocol}

\subsection{Cumulative node store}\label{sec:complete-store}

The final store is one cumulative object:
\[
\begin{split}
 \Sigma=(&t,s,\T,\timestamp[\cdot],\sigma[\cdot],
 \gfPool[\cdot],\attestations[\cdot],\\
 &F,J,h_j,\hmax,
 \liveConfirmed,\latestConfirmed).
\end{split}
\]
The local validator index is again~$\valindex$; the validator also keeps its
anti-slashing record
$\Lambda=(\mathsf{target}[\cdot],\mathsf{timeout}[\cdot],
\mathsf{lock}[\cdot])$ of Section~\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, one entry
per height, initially $(\bot,\mathsf{false},\bot)$.  It belongs to the
validator, not to the store, and $\Call{record\_attestation}{}$ is its
only writer.

$\Sigma.\attestations[r]$ holds the processed round-$r$ combined attestations.
An SG vote is an attestation's $(\valindex,\round,\confirmedf)$ projection,
and every SG rule and grade reads only this projection; the
pool keeps at most two attestations with distinct confirmed blocks per validator,
and attestations differing only in their pair fields are one vote.
Attestations reach the pool on the wire and through the node's own
$\Call{attest}{}$; an attestation resolves as an SG vote does
(\S\ref{sec:sg-store}), at the later of its own and its confirmed
block's timestamps---an empty confirmed block resolves at receipt---and
the pair fields never take part.
Attestations carried in a block are read by the state
transition alone and never enter the pool: Section~\ref{sec:sg-store}'s
rule that SG votes travel only on the wire is unchanged.

At initialization,
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \Sigma.t=\Sigma.s=0,\quad \Sigma.\T=\{\genesis\},\quad
 \Sigma.\timestamp(\genesis)=-\infty,\quad \Sigma.\hmax=1,\\
 \Sigma.F=\Sigma.J=\genesis,\quad \Sigma.h_j=0,\quad
 \Sigma.\liveConfirmed=\Sigma.\latestConfirmed=\genesis,
 \end{gathered}
\]
and every pool is empty.

The store does not cache a view, a quorum set, a support score, a grade,
an anchor, a viable tree, or a filtered tree: each is
derived when used, from timestamps compared against past instants.

\subsection{Duties and handlers}\label{sec:public-handlers}

The public times of the schedule, the view freeze included, are the
only tick times; the tick at a public time runs once, before the
objects processed at that time, so an object processed between two
public times carries the earlier one's stamp.
Figures~\ref{alg:store}--\ref{alg:chain-full} list the complete
protocol: every function in final form --- the store handlers, the
fork choice with its grades, the validator duties with their helpers,
the validator client, and the state transition --- each marked with the
section that defined it, unchanged means verbatim, and extended or
redefined marking the functions this layer changes.  The mathematical definitions they draw on --- resolution
times, scores and supports, grades, and the trees --- are those of
Sections~\ref{sec:goldfish}--\ref{sec:healing}, and at slot~$0$ the
head is genesis.  Relative to
the earlier layers, the proposer gains the carried attestations, block
processing gains the state transition, the finality update, and a
guard against reprocessing, and available confirmation now runs the
composed fork choice --- the filtered tree and the round's anchor ---
over the confirmation window's votes.  It advances only when the
result itself carries a window majority, and otherwise returns the
FG root: the confirmed value is always this evaluation's
output, either a genuine confirmation or the root, and the root sits
below every walk floor in $\Call{attest}{}$, so an unconfirmed slot
lands on the grade-2 and anchor fallbacks there.

\Function{on\_tick}{$\Sigma,\Lambda,t$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $s\gets\lfloor t/(4\Delta)\rfloor$; $(\Sigma.t,\Sigma.s)\gets(t,s)$
  \If{$s>0$ and $t=t_s$ and $\valindex$ is the slot-$s$ proposer}

  \EndIf

\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{update\_confirmation}{$\Sigma,s$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, redefined at this layer}
  \Comment{at $t_s+6\Delta$}
  \State $\mathit{early\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s]:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<t_s+2\Delta\}$
  \State $\mathit{late\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s]:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})<t_s+6\Delta\}$
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets
    \{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{early\_votes}:
    \mathit{late\_votes}\text{ holds no second distinct vote by }
    \mathit{vote}.\valindex\}$
  \State $\mathit{voters\_count}\gets
    |\{v:\mathit{late\_votes}\text{ holds a vote by }v\}|$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
    $2\,\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{support\_votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}>\mathit{voters\_count}$
  \State $\mathit{tree}\gets\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$;\quad
    $\mathit{root}\gets\Call{get\_fg\_root}{\Sigma}$
  \State $A\gets\Call{get\_sg\_root}{\Sigma,\round(\Sigma.s)}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{ghost}{A,\mathit{tree},
    \fname{goldfish\_score}(\mathit{support\_votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,\cdot),\mathit{eligible}}$
  \State $\Sigma.\liveConfirmed\gets H$ if $\mathit{eligible}(H)$,
    else $\mathit{root}$
  \If{$\Sigma.\latestConfirmed\preceq\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$}
    \State $\Sigma.\latestConfirmed\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_block}{$\Sigma,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, extended at this layer}
  \If{$B.\slot>\Sigma.s$ or $B\in\Sigma.\T$ or
      $B.\parent\notin\Sigma.\T$} \State \Return \EndIf
  \If{$\Sigma.F\not\preceq B$} \State \Return \EndIf
  \State $\Sigma.\sigma[B]\gets
    \Call{state\_transition}{\Sigma.\sigma[B.\parent],B}$
  \State add $B$ to $\Sigma.\T$; $\Sigma.\timestamp(B)\gets\Sigma.t$
  \ForAll{$\mathit{vote}\in B.\mathsf{votes}$}
    \State \Call{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \EndFor
  \State \Call{update\_finality}{$\Sigma,\Sigma.\sigma[B]$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, unchanged}
  \If{$\mathit{vote}.\slot<\Sigma.s-1$ or $\mathit{vote}.\slot>\Sigma.s$ or
    $\mathit{vote}.\valindex\notin\committee_{\mathit{vote}.\slot}$ or
    $\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$}
    \State \Return \EndIf
  \If{$\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$ holds two distinct votes by
    $\mathit{vote}.\valindex$}
    \State \Return \EndIf
  \State add $\mathit{vote}$ to $\Sigma.\gfPool[\mathit{vote}.\slot]$;
    $\Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})\gets\Sigma.t$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{on\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,a$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:majority-sg}, redefined at this layer}
  \State $\mathit{round\_votes}\gets\{b.\confirmedf:b\in\Sigma.\attestations[a.\round],\
    b.\valindex=a.\valindex\}$
  \If{$a.\round<\max\{0,\round(\Sigma.s)-\eta_{\rm SG}\}$ or
      $a.\round>\round(\Sigma.s)$ or
      $a.\confirmedf\in\mathit{round\_votes}$
      or $|\mathit{round\_votes}|=2$}
    \State \Return
  \EndIf
  \State add $a$ to $\Sigma.\attestations[a.\round]$;
    $\Sigma.\timestamp(a)\gets\Sigma.t$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{update\_finality}{$\Sigma,\sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $\Sigma.\hmax\gets\max(\Sigma.\hmax,\sigma.h)$
  \If{$\Sigma.F\preceq\sigma.J$ \textbf{and}
      $(\sigma.h_j,\sigma.J.\rootf)>(\Sigma.h_j,\Sigma.J.\rootf)$}
    \Comment{lex order}
    \State $(\Sigma.J,\Sigma.h_j)\gets(\sigma.J,\sigma.h_j)$
  \EndIf
  \If{$\Sigma.F\prec\sigma.F$ \textbf{and} $\sigma.F\preceq\Sigma.J$
      \textbf{and} $\sigma.F\in\Call{viable\_tree}{\Sigma}$}
    \State $\Sigma.F\gets\sigma.F$
  \EndIf
\EndFunction

  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, unchanged}

    \State $\mathit{children}\gets\{B:B\text{ is a child of }H\text{ in }
      \mathit{tree},\ \mathit{eligible}(B)\}$
    \If{$\mathit{children}=\emptyset$} \State \Return $H$ \EndIf
    \State $H\gets\arg\max_{B\in\mathit{children}}\mathit{score}(B)$,
      ties by root order
  \EndLoop
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_eligible}{$\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
  \mathit{support\_votes},s,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{voters\_count}\gets
    |\{v:\mathit{votes}\text{ holds a vote by }v\}|$
  \State \Return $\Sigma.\sigma[B.\parent].h<\Sigma.\hmax-1$
    or $2\,\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}>\mathit{voters\_count}$
    or $B.\slot=\Sigma.s$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_fork\_choice}{$\Sigma,\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
  \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, unchanged}
  \State define $\mathit{score}(B)$ as
  \Statex \hspace{2em}
    $\Call{goldfish\_score}{\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s,B}$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
  \Statex \hspace{2em}
    $\Call{goldfish\_eligible}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
      \mathit{support\_votes},s,B}$
  \State \Return $\Call{ghost}{\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
    \mathit{score},\mathit{eligible}}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{latest\_support\_vote}{$\Sigma,v,r$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:majority-sg}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{eligible}\gets\{r':\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\}\leq r'<r,\
    \exists a\in\Sigma.\attestations[r'],\ a.\valindex=v,\
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(a)<+\infty\}$
  \If{$\mathit{eligible}=\emptyset$} \State \Return $\bot$ \EndIf
  \State $r^*\gets\max\mathit{eligible}$
  \If{$\Sigma.\attestations[r^*]$ does not hold exactly one distinct SG vote by~$v$}
    \State \Return $\bot$
  \EndIf
  \State \Return that entry's SG-vote projection
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{sg\_support}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:majority-sg}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{supporters}\gets\emptyset$
  \ForAll{$v\in V$}
    \State $\mathit{vote}\gets\Call{latest\_support\_vote}{\Sigma,v,r}$
    \If{$\mathit{vote}=(v,r^*,C)$ with $C\neq\bot$ and $B\preceq C$}
      \State $\mathit{supporters}\gets\mathit{supporters}\cup\{v\}$
    \EndIf
  \EndFor
  \State \Return $w(\mathit{supporters})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{majority\_fork\_choice}{$\Sigma,\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},r$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:majority-sg}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{total}\gets
    w(\{v\in V:\exists r':\max\{0,r-\eta_{\rm SG}\}\leq r'<r,\
      \Sigma.\attestations[r']\text{ holds a vote by }v\})$
  \State define $\mathit{eligible}(B)$ as
    $2\,\Call{sg\_support}{\Sigma,r,B}>\mathit{total}$
  \State \Return $\Call{ghost}{\mathit{anchor},\mathit{tree},
    \fname{sg\_support}(\Sigma,r,\cdot),\mathit{eligible}}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_fg\_root}{$\Sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \If{$\Sigma.\hmax=\Sigma.h_j+1$} \State \Return $\Sigma.J$ \EndIf
  \State \Return $\Sigma.F$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_filtered\_block\_tree\_from}{$\Sigma,\mathit{blocks}$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{root}\gets\Call{get\_fg\_root}{\Sigma}$
  \State \Return
    $\{B\in\Call{viable\_tree\_from}{\Sigma,\mathit{blocks}}:
      \mathit{root}\preceq B\}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{$\Sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State \Return
    $\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree\_from}{\Sigma,\Sigma.\T}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{grade\_summary}{$\Sigma,r,v$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \If{$r=0$} \State \Return $(\bot,+\infty,+\infty)$ \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{fresh}\gets
    \{a\in\Sigma.\attestations[r-1]:
    a.\valindex=v\}$
  \State $\mathit{resolved}\gets\{a\in\mathit{fresh}:
    a.\confirmedf\neq\bot,\ \Sigma.\resolutionTime(a)<+\infty\}$
  \If{$\mathit{resolved}=\emptyset$}
    \State $(C_v,t_v)\gets(\bot,+\infty)$
  \Else
    \State $(t_v,C_v)\gets
      \min\{(\Sigma.\resolutionTime(a),a.\confirmedf):
      a\in\mathit{resolved}\}$
    \Comment{lexicographic; root order breaks ties}
  \EndIf
  \State $e_v\gets\max\{\Sigma.\timestamp(a):
    a\in\mathit{fresh}\}$ if
  \Statex \hspace{2em}$|\mathit{fresh}|>1$,
    else $+\infty$
  \State \Return $(C_v,t_v,e_v)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{direct\_support}{$\Sigma,r,\Gamma_h,\Gamma_e,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State for each $v\in V$, let
    $(C_v,t_v,e_v)\gets\Call{grade\_summary}{\Sigma,r,v}$
  \State \Return $w(\{v\in V:t_v<\Gamma_h,\ B\preceq C_v,\
    e_v\geq\Gamma_e\})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{favorable\_support}{$\Sigma,r,\Gamma,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State for each $v\in V$, let
    $(C_v,t_v,e_v)\gets\Call{grade\_summary}{\Sigma,r,v}$
  \State \Return $w(\{v\in V:t_v<\Gamma,\ B\preceq C_v\}
    \cup\{v\in V:e_v<\Gamma\})$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G2}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State \Return $\Call{direct\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^{-1},
    \Gamma_r^2,B}\geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G1}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State \Return $\Call{direct\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^0,
    \Gamma_r^0,B}\geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G0}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State \Return $\Call{favorable\_support}{\Sigma,r,\Gamma_r^1,B}
    \geq m$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{G0\_conflict\_free}{$\Sigma,r,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{vetoes}\gets
    \{B'\in\Sigma.T:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(B')<\Gamma_r^1,$
  \Statex \hspace{4.35em} $B'\text{ conflicts with }B,\
    \Call{G0}{\Sigma,r,B'}\}$
  \State \Return $\mathit{vetoes}=\emptyset$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_sg\_root}{$\Sigma,r$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, redefined in \S\ref{sec:healing}}
  \State $\mathit{tree}\gets\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$
  \If{some $B\in\mathit{tree}$ and
      $\Call{G1}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return the deepest such $B$
    \Comment{the fresh SG root}
  \ElsIf{some $B\in\Sigma.T$ and
      $\Call{G1}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return $\fname{get\_fg\_root}(\Sigma)$
    \Comment{grade~1 exists, but the root has passed it}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $\Call{majority\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,
    \fname{get\_fg\_root}(\Sigma),\mathit{tree},r}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_head\_in\_tree}{$\Sigma,\mathit{tree},
  \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},k$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $A\gets\Call{get\_sg\_root}{\Sigma,\round(\Sigma.s)}$
  \State \Return $\Call{goldfish\_fork\_choice}{\Sigma,A,
    \mathit{tree},\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},k}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_head}{$\Sigma,\mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},k$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{tree}\gets\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$
  \State \Return $\Call{get\_head\_in\_tree}{\Sigma,\mathit{tree},
    \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},k}$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

The vote duty uses a block-domain merge analogous to its vote merge.
It first forms a processed-block view from blocks received before the
previous-slot freeze and every ancestor of a current-slot proposal, then
recomputes viability and the filtered block tree inside that view.  In
particular, it does not filter the current viable tree after the fact: a
late witness cannot make an old pre-freeze child enter this vote's walk.
The proposer and the other protocol duties keep the ordinary full-tree
$\Call{get\_head}{}$.

\Function{voter\_processed\_block\_tree}{$\Sigma,s$}
  \State $\mathit{freeze}\gets t_{s-1}+3\Delta$
  \State $\mathit{proposals}\gets\{P\in\Sigma.\T:P.\slot=s\}$
  \State \Return $\{B\in\Sigma.\T:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(B)<\mathit{freeze}\ \textbf{or}\
    \exists P\in\mathit{proposals},\ B\preceq P\}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{voter\_filtered\_block\_tree}{$\Sigma,s$}
  \State $\mathit{blocks}\gets
    \Call{voter\_processed\_block\_tree}{\Sigma,s}$
  \State \Return
    $\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree\_from}{\Sigma,\mathit{blocks}}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{propose\_block}{$\Sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, extended at this layer}
  \State $s\gets\Sigma.s$
  \Comment{runs at $t_s$}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\Sigma.\gfPool[s-1]$
  \Comment{every previous-slot vote held}
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<+\infty\}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},s-1}$
  \State $B\gets$ a block with $B.\parent=H$, $B.\slot=s$,
    $B.\proposer=\valindex$
  \State $B.\mathsf{votes}\gets\mathit{votes}$
  \State $B.\mathsf{support\_votes}\gets\mathit{support\_votes}$
    \Comment{the proposal-time support subset}
  \State $B.\mathsf{attestations}\gets$ every attestation~$a$ with
    $\Sigma.\resolutionTime(a)<+\infty$ that is not on $H$'s chain
  \State broadcast $B$; \Call{on\_block}{$\Sigma,B$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:goldfish}, unchanged}
  \State $s\gets\Sigma.s$
  \Comment{runs at $t_s+\Delta$}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\Sigma.\gfPool[s-1]:
    \Sigma.\timestamp(\mathit{vote})<t_{s-1}+3\Delta\}$
    \Comment{received before the freeze}
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<t_{s-1}+3\Delta\}$
  \ForAll{$B\in\Sigma.\T$ with $B.\slot=s$}
    \State $\mathit{included\_votes}\gets
      \{\mathit{vote}\in B.\mathsf{votes}:\mathit{vote}.\slot=s-1\}$
    \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\mathit{votes}\cup\mathit{included\_votes}$
    \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\mathit{support\_votes}\cup
      \{(v,s-1,H)\in B.\mathsf{support\_votes}:H\in\Sigma.\T\}$
  \EndFor
  \State $\mathit{tree}\gets
    \Call{voter\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma,s}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head\_in\_tree}{\Sigma,\mathit{tree},
    \mathit{votes},\mathit{support\_votes},s-1}$
  \If{$\valindex\in\committee_s$}
    \State $\mathit{vote}\gets(\valindex,s,H)$; broadcast $\mathit{vote}$;
      \Call{on\_goldfish\_vote}{$\Sigma,\mathit{vote}$}
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{attest}{$\Sigma,\Lambda$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State $r\gets\round(\Sigma.s)$
  \Comment{runs at $a_r$}
  \State $\Agtwo\gets$ the deepest
    $B\in\Call{get\_filtered\_block\_tree}{\Sigma}$ with
    $\Call{G2}{\Sigma,r,B}$,
    else $\bot$
  \State $\Csg\gets\Call{get\_sg\_vote}{\Sigma,r,\Agtwo}$
  \State $(h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F)\gets
    \Call{get\_fg\_vote}{\Sigma,r,\Agtwo}$
  \State $a\gets\Call{create\_attestation}{\Lambda,r,\Csg,
    h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F}$
  \State broadcast $a$; \Call{on\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,a$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_sg\_vote}{$\Sigma,r,\Agtwo$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State $C\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$;\quad
    $A\gets\Call{get\_sg\_root}{\Sigma,r}$
  \If{some $B$ with $A\preceq B\preceq C$ satisfies
      $\Call{G0\_conflict\_free}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return the deepest such $B$
  \EndIf
  \If{$\Agtwo\neq\bot$}
    \State \Return $\Agtwo$
    \Comment{the grade-2 block stays canonical}
  \ElsIf{some $B\in\Sigma.T$ and $\Call{G2}{\Sigma,r,B}$}
    \State \Return $\Call{get\_fg\_root}{\Sigma}$
    \Comment{grade~2 exists, but the root has passed it}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $A$
  \Comment{the anchor fallback keeps voting total}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{get\_fg\_vote}{$\Sigma,r,\Agtwo$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:healing}, unchanged}
  \State $\mathit{votes}\gets\Sigma.\gfPool[\Sigma.s]$
  \State $\mathit{support\_votes}\gets\{\mathit{vote}\in\mathit{votes}:
    \Sigma.\resolutionTime(\mathit{vote})<+\infty\}$
  \State $H\gets\Call{get\_head}{\Sigma,\mathit{votes},
    \mathit{support\_votes},\Sigma.s}$
  \State $(h_j,J,h_F)\gets(\Sigma.\sigma[H].h_j,\Sigma.\sigma[H].J,
    \Sigma.\sigma[H].h_F)$
  \If{$\Agtwo=\bot$}
    \State \Return $(\bot,\bot,\bot,h_j,J,h_F)$
    \Comment{no fresh quorum: no height pair}
  \EndIf
  \State $C\gets\Sigma.\liveConfirmed$
  \State $\Cfg\gets$ the deepest $B$ with $\Agtwo\preceq B\preceq C$
    and $\Call{G0\_conflict\_free}{\Sigma,r,B}$, else $\Agtwo$
  \State \Return $(\Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].h,\Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].T_h,
    \Sigma.\sigma[\Cfg].\nj,h_j,J,h_F)$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\Function{finality\_pair}{$\Lambda,h_j,J,h_F$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \If{$h_j>h_F$}
    \Comment{a justification not yet finalized}
    \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_j]\in\{\bot,J\}$ and
        not $\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h_j]$ and
        $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_j]\in\{\bot,J\}$}
      \Comment{the record allows it}
      \State \Return $(h_j,J)$
    \EndIf
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{height\_pair}{$\Lambda,h_c,T_c,\nu,h_f,T_f$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \If{$h_c=\bot$} \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$ \EndIf
  \State $\mathit{lock}\gets\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_c]$;\quad
    \textbf{if} $h_c=h_f$ \textbf{then} $\mathit{lock}\gets T_f$
  \Comment{this attestation's own lock}
  \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h_c]$}
    \State \Return $(h_c,\bot)$
    \Comment{a timeout repeats}
  \EndIf
  \If{$\mathit{lock}\neq\bot$}
    \Comment{a lock repeats}
    \If{$\mathit{lock}=T_c$}
      \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \EndIf
    \State \Return $(\bot,\bot)$
    \Comment{locked elsewhere: wait out the height}
  \EndIf
  \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_c]\neq\bot$}
    \Comment{a recorded target repeats}
    \If{$\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h_c]=T_c$}
      \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \EndIf
  \ElsIf{not $\nu$}
    \State \Return $(h_c,T_c)$
    \Comment{no history: adopt the chain's target}
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $(h_c,\bot)$
  \Comment{otherwise time out at $h_c$}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{create\_attestation}{$\Lambda,r,\mathit{confirmed},
  h_c,T_c,\nu,h_j,J,h_F$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \Comment{validator-client side: no store access}
  \State $(h_f,T_f)\gets\Call{finality\_pair}{\Lambda,h_j,J,h_F}$
  \State $(h,T)\gets\Call{height\_pair}{\Lambda,h_c,T_c,\nu,h_f,T_f}$
  \State $a\gets(\valindex,r,\mathit{confirmed},h,T,h_f,T_f)$
  \State \Call{record\_attestation}{$\Lambda,a$}; \Return $a$
  \Comment{record, then release}
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{record\_attestation}{$\Lambda,a$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:fg-fork-choice}, unchanged}
  \Comment{the only writer of $\Lambda$}
  \State $(h,T,h_f,T_f)\gets a$'s height and finality pairs
  \If{$T_f\neq\bot$} \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{lock}[h_f]\gets T_f$ \EndIf
  \If{$h\neq\bot$}
    \If{$T\neq\bot$ and $\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h]=\bot$}
      \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{target}[h]\gets T$
    \EndIf
    \If{$T=\bot$}
      \State $\Lambda.\mathsf{timeout}[h]\gets\mathsf{true}$
    \EndIf
  \EndIf
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
}

\Function{state\_transition}{$\sigma,B$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:state-machine}, unchanged}
  \State \textbf{assert} $B.\proposer=\operatorname{proposer}(B.\slot)$
  \State \textbf{assert} $B.\parent.\slot<B.\slot$
  \State $\sigma.s\gets B.\slot$
  \ForAll{$a\in B.\mathsf{attestations}$}
    \State $\sigma\gets\Call{process\_attestation}{\sigma,a}$
  \EndFor
  \State $\sigma.L\gets B$
  \State \Return $\Call{process\_height\_events}{\sigma}$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{process\_attestation}{$\sigma,a$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:state-machine}, unchanged}
  \State $\valindex\gets a.\valindex$
  \If{$\sigma.h_j>\sigma.h_F$
      \textbf{and} $a$'s finality pair $=(\sigma.h_j,\sigma.J)$}
    \State $\sigma.\finalityBit[\valindex]\gets\mathsf{true}$
  \EndIf
  \If{$a$'s height pair $=(\sigma.h,\sigma.T_h)$}
    \State $\sigma.\targetParticipation[\valindex]\gets\mathsf{true}$
    \State $\sigma.\progressBit[\valindex]\gets\mathsf{true}$
  \ElsIf{$a$'s height pair $=(\sigma.h,\bot)$}
    \State $\sigma.\progressBit[\valindex]\gets\mathsf{true}$
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $\sigma$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{process\_height\_events}{$\sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:state-machine}, unchanged}
  \If{$\sigma.h_j>\sigma.h_F$
      \textbf{and} $w(\Qfin(\sigma))\geq q$}
    \State $(\sigma.F,\sigma.h_F)\gets(\sigma.J,\sigma.h_j)$
  \EndIf
  \If{$w(\Qtarget(\sigma))\geq q$}
    \If{$\neg\sigma.\nj$}
      \State $(\sigma.J,\sigma.h_j)\gets(\sigma.T_h,\sigma.h)$
      \State $\sigma.\finalityBit\gets\mathsf{false}^{V}$
    \EndIf
    \State \Return $\Call{advance\_height}{\sigma}$
  \EndIf
  \If{$w(\Qprog(\sigma))\geq q$ \textbf{and}
      $\sigma.s\geq\sigma.T_h.\slot+\delta_{\mathrm{t}}$}
    \Comment{timeouts count only after the delay}
    \State \Return $\Call{advance\_height}{\sigma}$
  \EndIf
  \State \Return $\sigma$
\EndFunction
\vspace{1pt}
\Function{advance\_height}{$\sigma$}
  \Comment{\S\ref{sec:state-machine}, unchanged}
  \State $\sigma.h\gets\sigma.h+1$; \quad $\sigma.T_h\gets\sigma.L$
  \State $\sigma.\nj\gets(K\mid\sigma.h)\wedge
    (\sigma.h-\sigma.h_F>D)$
  \State $\sigma.\targetParticipation,
    \sigma.\progressBit\gets\mathsf{false}^{V}$
  \State \Return $\sigma$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
