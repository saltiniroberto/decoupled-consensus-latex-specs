This repository contains notes and specifications for finality gadgets.
Each protocol spec lives under \texttt{full/} as a standalone \LaTeX{} file
and produces two PDFs:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a \emph{full} version (e.g.\ \texttt{full/\textit{name}.pdf}) including
    lemmas, corollaries, propositions, properties, proofs, remarks, and
    assumptions;
  \item a \emph{lite} version (\texttt{\textit{name}\_lite.pdf}) built from a
    thin wrapper at the repository root which sets a flag and \texttt{\textbackslash input}s the full
    file. The lite build drops the theorem-like environments above and keeps
    only algorithms, definitions, theorem statements, and surrounding prose;
    its title is annotated with ``(Lite)''.
\end{itemize}
The shared exclusion logic lives in \texttt{full/lite\_full\_setup.tex}.

\begin{description}

\item[\texttt{height\_filter\_and\_timeouts.tex}]
  \fbox{\textbf{\large Closest to becoming the candidate protocol.}}\\
  \textbf{Fresh Simplex with Height Filter and Timeouts.}
  Uses a finality-gadget height counter (separate from slot) with two
  height-advancing mechanisms -- fixed-target justification and timeouts --
  both gated by a height-freshness rule on vote targets.
  Covers accountable safety, the fork-choice store
  (with local irreversibility, lock-in, and order independence),
  the inactivity leak (penalty units, leak tightness, honest voting rule,
  leak fairness), healing from asynchrony with view-merge,
  and a per-round liveness argument over slot ranges (WIP).

\item[\texttt{prefix\_height\_filter\_and\_timeouts.tex}]
  \textbf{Fresh Simplex with Height Filter and Prefix Justification.}
  Merges \texttt{height\_filter\_and\_timeouts.tex} with the prefix-counting and
  slashing mechanisms of \texttt{finalization\_as\_notarization.tex}: both
  justification and finalization are \emph{prefix} counts, so votes on the same
  chain naming different (comparable) targets combine, and a finalize vote
  doubles as a timeout so a validator that has committed to finalize a height
  still counts as active there. Slashing reduces to three conditions -- one
  justify and one finalize target per height, and no timeout at a height where
  a finalize was committed. Covers accountable safety, the fork-choice store,
  and the inactivity leak (leak tightness and view-independent Layer-1 fairness).

\item[\texttt{finalization\_as\_notarization.tex}] \textbf{Finalization as Notarization.}
  Defines a finality gadget with three vote types (notarize, justify, finalize)
  and a quorum-intersection slashing condition.
  Covers accountable safety, the fork-choice store, the inactivity leak,
  and a top-down notarization variant.
  This is the most current document; definitions and proofs are
  internally consistent, though some lemma sketches remain in the
  inactivity-leak section.

\item[\texttt{portable\_finalization.tex}] \textbf{Portable Finalization.}
  Specifies a finality gadget in which justification certificates are
  portable across forks (two vote types: prefix and finalize).
  Includes accountable-safety proofs, the fork-choice store,
  the inactivity-leak mechanism, and a comparison with Prefix-IC.
  Predates \texttt{finalization\_as\_notarization.tex};
  the model and terminology have since diverged and are not fully
  up to date.

\item[\texttt{rooted\_timeouts.tex}] \textbf{Rooted Timeouts.}
  Specifies a finality gadget with primary/secondary votes, rooted timeouts,
  and a slot-based monotonicity mechanism.
  Covers accountable safety, the fork-choice store,
  the inactivity leak, and a comparison with Simplex.
  An earlier draft with several open review comments and unresolved
  design questions; the model and terminology have since diverged.

\item[\texttt{fresh\_simplex\_with\_notarizations.tex}]
  \textbf{Fresh Simplex with Prefix Notarizations.}
  Drops timeouts and snapshots; uses fixed-target justification and
  prefix notarization as the two height-advancing mechanisms, both
  gated by a height-freshness rule on vote targets.
  The fork-choice root is the lex-max over justification and notarization
  certificates, with justifications preferred on same-height ties.
  Covers accountable safety, the fork-choice store (root/finalized pair),
  and the inactivity leak (explicit honest voting rule, leak fairness,
  amortized tightness).

\end{description}
